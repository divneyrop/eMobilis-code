How I Work

The principles guiding how I learn, experiment, and approach technical problems.

I learn best by building something real, testing it, and debugging what breaks—not by stopping at a tutorial.

Learn by Building

Working Principle

Data cleaning, assumptions, and evaluation are part of the problem—not chores to rush through before modelling.

Respect the Data

Working Principle

Accuracy alone can hide important mistakes. I pay attention to precision, recall, F1-score, and the cost of different errors.

Evaluate Honestly

Working Principle

Readable code matters. I aim to make my work understandable to collaborators—and to my future self.

Build for Clarity

Working Principle

I want to understand how tools fit together, from a web interface and backend to Linux and the operating system.

Understand the Stack

Working Principle

I start with a real question, keep the scope manageable, and improve the work through evidence, feedback, and iteration.
